Install Gap first, then Streak

Follow the steps for your device. The page then takes you to Streak.

Gap→Streak

iPhone or iPad

Safari
  1. 1Tap Share . Don’t see it? Tap ··· next to the address bar first.
  2. 2Tap Add to Home Screen . Scroll down if you don’t see it.
  3. 3Keep Open as Web App on, check the name says Gap, then tap Add.
Chrome on iPhone works too, on iOS 16.4 or later.

Android phone

Chrome
  1. 1Tap Install Gap on the page, then Install.
  2. 2No button? Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. 3If asked, choose Install, not “Create shortcut”, and confirm.

Windows or Mac

Chrome or Edge
  1. 1Click Install Gap on the page, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. 2No button? Use the browser menu:
    Chrome⋮›Cast, save, and share›Install page as app
    Edge···›Apps›Install this site as an app
  3. 3Click Install. Gap opens in its own window.

Mac

Safari
  1. 1In the menu bar, click File›Add to Dock. You’ll also find it under Share .
  2. 2Check the name says Gap and click Add.
  3. 3Open Gap from the Dock from now on, not from a Safari tab.
Needs macOS Sonoma 14 or later. On an older Mac, use Chrome.

Now Streak. When Gap is installed (on iPhone and Safari, tap I’ve added Gap on the page), it shows Now install Streak. Tap it and repeat the steps; the name should say Streak. Done when you see two icons, Gap and Streak.

Then, a few good habits

Open from the icons

Enter your numbers in Gap and Streak from their icons, not a browser tab. On iPhone, iPad and Safari on Mac, a tab keeps separate data.

Back up each app weekly

Gap and Streak keep separate records, so back up each one: Settings › Save a backup file. Also after setup and big changes.

Private by design

Your entries stay on your device; nothing is sent to us. Encrypted backup adds a passphrase to the file. Keep it safe.

Coming from the older one-window version? Save a backup there first, then use Settings › Restore from a backup.
