How your data is handled

Nothing is collected. BeFree Streak has no server, no account and no login. Every habit, tick and to-do is written to your own browser storage on this device and stays there.

Nothing is sent anywhere. The app makes no network calls once it has loaded. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no bank connection, no third party. Even the fonts are built into the file, which is why it works with the internet switched off.

Streak works on its own. It does not read or share records with BeFree Gap or any other app, and nothing travels between devices.

Nobody can see it, including us. If you clear your browser data it is gone and cannot be recovered by anyone, so save a backup from Settings now and then and keep it somewhere you trust.
