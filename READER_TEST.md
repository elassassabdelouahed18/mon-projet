# Reader test — five readers, fourteen days

An internal quality test of the BeFree bundle. It measures whether the system
works in someone else's hands. **It is not a source of testimonials, quotes or
marketing material**, and nothing a reader says here goes on a sales page.

---

## The question

Not "did they like it". The question is whether a reader who has never seen
this material can, on their own:

1. find their real number,
2. say where they are and what they do next,
3. still be doing it two weeks later.

## The pass mark

**Four of the five readers**, on all three:

| | What has to happen | How it is measured |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Their **real gap** is on the screen in Gap within **15 minutes** of opening the installation sheet | you time it, from first tap to the Insights number being theirs and not the sample's |
| **2** | They can **name their phase** and **their next step** without looking it up | ask on day 1 and again on day 14; "I'm at Map, my next step is the automatic transfer" passes, "er, budgeting?" does not |
| **3** | They are **still reviewing on day 14** | Gap shows at least one entry they logged themselves in the last three days, and at least one weekly review |

A reader who fails on time but passes on the other two is a fail. The fifteen
minutes is the promise the install sheet makes.

## Who to ask

Five people who are **not** friends of the project and who would be a plausible
buyer. Aim for this spread, and do not take five of the same kind:

| | Who | Why they are in the test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paid **hourly**, pay changes week to week | the core reader; Appendix B exists for them |
| 2 | **Salaried**, comfortable income, no gap | the Maya case: lifestyle creep, not shortage |
| 3 | **Negative gap right now**, and knows it | tests whether *When the Math Doesn't Close* lands before anything else does |
| 4 | **Side income** already, no system around it | tests Part Three and the tax reserve |
| 5 | **Not comfortable with apps.** Android or an old iPhone | tests the installation sheet, which is where most failures will be |

Never test with someone who already uses a budgeting app without saying so in
the notes: they will pass step 1 on prior knowledge.

## What you give them

The bundle as it ships, and nothing else:

- `Installation-Guide-Gap-and-Streak.pdf` (one page)
- `Start-Here-The-BeFree-System.pdf`, or the phone-format file on a phone
- `The-Anti-Paycheck-Trap.pdf`, or the EPUB on a phone

**Do not explain anything.** If they ask you a question, write the question
down and answer only after the fifteen minutes are up. The questions are the
most valuable thing the test produces.

---

## The fourteen days

**Day 0, with them, 30 minutes.**
Hand over the three files. Start a timer when they open the install sheet.
Write down, with timestamps:

- when both apps are installed;
- when the first real number of theirs appears;
- **every question they ask**, in their words;
- every place they stop, scroll back, or look for something that is not there;
- whether they open the book at all, and where they start.

Stop the timer when their own gap is on the screen. Note the time even if it
is over fifteen minutes.

**Day 1, a message.** "What is your phase, and what is your next step?"
Write the answer down verbatim.

**Day 7, a message.** "Still going? Anything in the way?" Nothing else.

**Day 14, 20 minutes.** Ask again for the phase and the next step. Look at the
app with them: entries in the last three days, a weekly review, a habit ticked.
Then one open question, and let it run: **"What would have made this easier?"**

---

## What to record

One file per reader, four fields and nothing clever:

```
Reader 3 · hourly, negative gap
Minutes to their own number:        22      FAIL
Phase and next step, day 1:         "Stabilize. Stop the overdrafts."   PASS
Phase and next step, day 14:        "Map. The transfer on payday."      PASS
Still reviewing on day 14:          yes, 11 entries, 2 reviews          PASS
Questions asked:
  00:04  "Do I have to type every coffee?"
  00:09  "Which one is Gap again?"
  00:17  "Is this my money or the example's?"
Where they stopped:
  the category list on first log; scrolled it twice
What would have made it easier:
  "Tell me on the first screen that the numbers I see are fake."
```

The last line in that example is worth more than the three PASS marks above it.

## Reading the result

- **Four or five pass** — ship it, and fix whatever the questions point at.
- **Three pass** — do not ship. Find what the two failures share. If both
  failed on time, the installation sheet or the first setup screen is the
  problem, and that is a fixable, specific thing.
- **Two or fewer** — the problem is not polish.

Count a question asked by **three or more readers** as a defect in the
products, whatever the pass marks say. Three people asking the same thing is
not three people being slow.

## What this test does not do

It does not measure whether the system works financially. Fourteen days is not
long enough to widen a gap, and nobody in this test should be told it is. It
measures whether a person can start and keep going, which is the only thing the
bundle can be held responsible for in two weeks.
