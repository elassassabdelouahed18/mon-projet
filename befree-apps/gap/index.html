How your data is handled

Nothing is collected. BeFree Gap has no server, no account and no login. Every amount, category, goal and debt you enter is written to your own browser storage on this device and stays there.

Nothing is sent anywhere. The app makes no network calls once it has loaded. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no bank connection and no third party of any kind. Even the fonts are built into the file, which is why it works with the internet switched off.

Gap works on its own. It does not share your records with BeFree Streak or any other app, and nothing travels between devices.

Nobody can see it, including us. BeFree has no way to read your figures, because they never leave your device. If you clear your browser data, the entries are gone and cannot be recovered by anyone, so save a backup from Settings now and then and keep it somewhere you trust.

You can erase everything. The reset button in Settings deletes every entry on this device permanently.
